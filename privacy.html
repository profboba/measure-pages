Measure privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

What Measure reads

With the owner’s permission, Measure reads the owner’s own Google Health records through read-only access to four kinds of data: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, and the account settings it needs to place records in time (such as the time zone). Measure cannot change or delete anything in the Google account.

Where the data goes

Measure keeps the records only on the owner’s Mac. It has no server, and it uses no advertising or analytics. It does not sell Google user data, and does not transfer it to anyone, with one exception made only at the owner’s explicit request: the owner may select records and ask an AI assistant they use, such as ChatGPT or Claude, about them. Measure does not use Google user data to train AI models.

Measure’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and removing data

The records stay on the owner’s Mac until the owner deletes them. Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account’s third-party connections.

Contact

Questions go to the support email shown on Google’s sign-in consent screen for Measure.
